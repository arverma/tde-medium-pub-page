Edit for Towards Data Engineering

We are looking for data engineers and experienced editors who can review submissions and make technical stories clearer. Editors are part of Medium's Editor Partner Program (EPP), which rewards publication editors for improving stories.

What editors do

  • Review writer submissions for clarity, accuracy and fit
  • Provide feedback to help authors improve technical writing
  • Approve stories for publication on Towards Data Engineering
  • Earn compensation through Medium's Editor Partner Program

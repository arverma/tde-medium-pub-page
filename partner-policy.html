Partner Content at TDE

We work with a small number of partners on useful, clearly disclosed content. Here's how we approach it.

Scope

  • We're open to partner content that teaches practitioners something useful about data engineering, AI infrastructure, or analytics engineering.
  • Partner content is never published on our Medium publication. It appears only on tde.arverma.dev, in the TDE newsletter, on Discord, or at live events.

Disclosure

  • Every paid piece carries a clear label at the top: “Partner content · Paid by [Brand]”.
  • We follow the FTC Endorsement Guides and disclose material connections in plain language.

Quality bar

  • The strongest pieces offer hands-on depth with working code or architecture, rather than reading like a product brochure.
  • Editors may suggest rewrites or decide that a piece is not the right fit; payment does not determine the editorial decision.
  • Product claims should be supportable, with no deceptive comparisons.

Author consent

  • If a partner piece carries a named author's byline, that author must agree in writing to its paid nature.
  • Our Medium authors are never required to promote a sponsor.

What we avoid

  • We don't take on undisclosed paid posts, paid queue-jumping on Medium, scraped or AI-spam drafts, or anything that conflicts with Medium's rules or the law.

Contact

  • Have an idea? Email towardsdataengg@gmail.com with your requirements and we'll connect to explore what we can do together.
